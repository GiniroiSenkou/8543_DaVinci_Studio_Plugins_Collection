SafeZones - DaVinci Resolve Edit effect (Fusion macro). Version 2026-09-26.
Turns the monitor into a phone screen and draws the real app interface on it for
Instagram Reel / Story, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reel / Story, LinkedIn Video
and Insta360 Community, in four views: full-screen, feed scroll, comments open and
profile grid (dark or light). Your video is cut to the Upload shape (9:16, 4:5, 1:1,
3:4, 16:9 - never squeezed) and placed the way the app shows it; vertical or horizontal
timelines. Optional guides: covered areas, safe box, feed/grid crop lines.
Put it on an adjustment clip; turn it off before rendering. One quick effect + icon per app.
Look at: README.md, src/Giniroisenkou_SafeZones_gen.py (screens, DANGER/CROPS tables),
src/Giniroisenkou_SafeZones_macro.py (controls and maths).
